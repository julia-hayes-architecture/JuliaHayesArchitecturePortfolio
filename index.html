03 / Design Philosophy

"Architecture should speak of its time and places, but yearn for timelessness." - Frank Gehry

I believe meaningful architecture exists at the intersection of beauty, functionality, and human experience. A successful space should not only serve a purpose, but also create an atmosphere, encourage interaction, and evoke a sense of belonging. I am particularly interested in the balance between modern simplicity and thoughtful detail, exploring how natural light, materials, proportion, and spatial relationships contribute to an evironment. My design philosophy continues to develop through observation, experimentation, and creative exploration.
